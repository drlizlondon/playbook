# Report shape

`scripts/angles.mjs` renders this. The structure is fixed so runs are comparable across months and products — do not rearrange it by hand, and do not delete the parts that make a thin run look thin.

> a claim about prevalence.

## Rules for reading it

**The header is the honesty check.** Read it before the angles. Ten signals across two sources is a different document from ten signals across nine, and the header is where that shows.

**A thin run should produce fewer angles, not weaker ones.** If under three clear the bar, the report says so in place of promoting filler. Widen the sources and re-run instead.

**Counter-signals are not caveats.** They are the reason to trust the rest. An angle whose counter-signal is missing was not compiled; an angle whose counter-signal is vague was not thought about.

**The uncertainty block is generated, not written.** It appears whenever confidence is `thin`, `single-source` or `hypothesis`. Do not remove it when pasting the report elsewhere — that is the one edit that turns this document back into the thing it exists to prevent.

## Automatic evidence gaps

The compiler adds gaps of its own to whatever the analyst recorded:

- a theme resting on a single source
- a run with no quantitative or internal signal anywhere, meaning nothing in it can speak to prevalence
- fewer than three angles clearing the test-first threshold
